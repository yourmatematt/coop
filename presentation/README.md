# Mallacoota Beauty Pivot - Pitch Deck

Professional PowerPoint presentation for Charles (CEO of Mallacoota Black Lip Abalone Co-Operative) presenting a beauty/wellness market repositioning strategy.

## Overview

This presentation repositions Mallacoota's wild black lip abalone from a commodity food product to a premium beauty/wellness ingredient, leveraging existing cultural beliefs about abalone's health benefits while adding scientific validation.

## Key Strategic Elements

### Stage-Gate Approach
- **Gate 1:** Lab testing ($15-25K) - Prove 30%+ compound superiority
- **Gate 2:** Legal review ($5-10K) - Validate permissible claims
- **Gate 3:** Micro-influencer test ($10-15K) - Test market resonance
- **Gate 4:** Limited launch ($30-50K) - 5K-10K units premium line
- **Gate 5:** Scale ($50-100K) - Multi-market expansion

Total investment: $110-200K with GO/NO-GO decisions at each stage.

### Financial Projections
- **Year 1:** $1.2-1.8M revenue (20-30K units)
- **Year 2:** $3-5M revenue (50-80K units)
- **Year 3:** $6-10M revenue (100-150K units)
- **Margins:** 60% gross (vs 30-40% commodity)
- **ROI:** 1,800-3,173% over 3 years

### Target Market
- Gen Z/Millennial beauty consumers in Singapore, Malaysia, Hong Kong
- Influencer-driven discovery channel
- "Eating your way to beauty" cultural trend
- Clean beauty movement (hormone-free, wild-sourced)

## Presentation Strategy

This deck is designed for board presentation to:
1. Establish the severity of the crisis (slides 2-3)
2. Present the strategic insight (slides 4-5)
3. Detail the risk-managed implementation (slides 6-10)
4. Demonstrate financial viability (slides 11-13)
5. Show clear execution path (slides 14-16)
6. Drive decision for Gate 1 approval (slide 17)

The presentation emphasizes controlled risk through stage-gates, making it easier for the board to approve the initial $15-25K investment rather than committing to the full strategy upfront.

## Key Talking Points

1. **Crisis is existential:** 75% revenue collapse in 2 years
2. **Traditional strategies won't work:** Resource constraints eliminate chef programs, retail marketing
3. **Category shift is the answer:** Not competing as food, but as beauty ingredient
4. **Cultural beliefs already exist:** Just need scientific proof
5. **Risk is managed:** GO/NO-GO at every gate, can exit anytime
6. **Financial upside is substantial:** $6-10M vs current $1.5M trajectory
7. **First decision point in 8 weeks:** Lab results will validate or invalidate quickly
